Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and act like humans. 
It encompasses a wide range of technologies that enable computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and decision-making. 
AI is not a single technology, but rather a collection of techniques and approaches used to create systems that can analyze data, identify patterns, and make predictions.
Key aspects of AI:
Learning:
AI systems can learn from data, identify patterns, and improve their performance over time without explicit programming for each task. 
Reasoning and Problem Solving:
AI can analyze information, identify solutions, and make decisions based on logical reasoning. 
Adaptability:
AI systems can adjust to new inputs and situations, making them suitable for dynamic environments. 
Types of AI:
Narrow or Weak AI: Focused on performing specific tasks, like image recognition or playing a game.
General or Strong AI: Hypothetical AI with human-level intelligence, capable of performing any intellectual task that a human being can.
Super AI: Hypothetical AI with intelligence exceeding that of humans.
